During the official live launch of "স্মার্ট স্টার্ট: HSC + AI" hosted by EdTech leader Shikho, Foysal Mahmud joined as 1 of 3 invited Notre Dame College merit students alongside host Shahedin. The broadcast delivered actionable academic guidelines for junior SSC-to-HSC students—focusing on bridging foundational science concepts, managing rigorous study schedules, and utilizing artificial intelligence as an ethical, personalized learning accelerator.

01 // Academic Framework

Key Guidelines Shared on the Live Broadcast

A structured synthesis of the study methodologies and AI productivity frameworks presented during the Shikho panel.

PILLAR 01

SSC to HSC Transition Roadmap

Deconstructing the qualitative jump in syllabus breadth from SSC to HSC. Prioritizing foundational physics mechanics, chemical bonding, and calculus principles within the initial 90 days.

PILLAR 02

Ethical AI as a Study Accelerator

Leveraging AI models for personalized Socratic questioning, generating custom practice problem sets, breaking down complex textbook mechanisms, and rapid doubt resolution.

PILLAR 03

Active Recall & Revision Loops

Implementing spaced repetition algorithms and active recall strategies to systematically retain large volumes of scientific terminology and formulas without passive rereading.

PILLAR 04

Consistency & Exam Discipline

Maintaining psychological resilience through disciplined daily problem-solving quotas, simulated mock test timing, and systematic error tracking notebooks.

03 // FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information regarding Foysal Mahmud's featured guest appearance on Shikho Live.

What was the official title of the Shikho Live event?
The event was the official live launch of "স্মার্ট স্টার্ট: HSC + AI" (Smart Start: HSC + AI) organized and broadcasted live by Shikho.
What was Foysal Mahmud's role on the Shikho live panel?
Foysal Mahmud was featured as 1 of 3 Special Guest Merit Students from Notre Dame College, Dhaka, invited to share practical academic advice, study methodologies, and AI integration strategies for junior students entering HSC.
How does Foysal Mahmud advocate using AI in academic studies?
Foysal advocates using AI as a 24/7 intelligent study tutor for clarifying difficult textbook concepts, generating practice problems, organizing revision schedules, and diagnosing weak academic areas—while maintaining strict academic integrity and personal problem-solving discipline.
