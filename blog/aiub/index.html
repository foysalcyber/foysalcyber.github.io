OFFICIAL EVENT ARCHIVE // 22 NOVEMBER 2025

Workshop on Robotics for NDSC at AIUB

On 22nd November 2025, the Office of Student Affairs (OSA), AIUB and the AIUB Robotic Crew (ARC) organized a specialized full-day Workshop on Robotics for 66 students of Notre Dame College Science Club (NDSC). The program combined advanced 3D CAD modeling, live drone aerodynamics, hands-on microcontrollers, and a thrilling 16-player Robo Soccer championship.

02 // Workshop Structure

Key Segments of the AIUB Robotics Day

A step-by-step overview of the interactive training and competitive activities on 22 November 2025.

SEGMENT 01

Technical Masterclass & CAD

Comprehensive orientation on microcontroller architectures, sensor arrays, motor control algorithms, and 3D SolidWorks CAD modeling by Dr. Ebad Zahir.

SEGMENT 02

16-Player Robo Soccer Tournament

16 selected Notre Dame College members went head-to-head in a fast-paced Robo Soccer competition testing maneuvering speed and mechanical durability.

Prize & Medal Ceremony

Champion and Runners-Up received Trophies, while all 66 NDSC participants were presented with Medals and Certificates by university leadership.

03 // FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct facts regarding the Notre Dame College Science Club robotics workshop at AIUB.

What was the date and venue of the NDSC Robotics Workshop at AIUB?
The workshop took place on 22nd November 2025 at the central campus and auditorium of American International University–Bangladesh (AIUB) in Kuratoli, Dhaka.
Who organized the robotics workshop at AIUB?
The workshop was organized by the Office of Student Affairs (OSA), AIUB with active technical support and coordination from the AIUB Robotic Crew (ARC) for the students of Notre Dame College Science Club (NDSC).
What awards were given at the conclusion of the workshop?
The Champion and Runners-Up of the Robo Soccer competition were awarded championship Trophies, and all 66 participating students received official Medals and Appreciation Certificates from AIUB leadership.
